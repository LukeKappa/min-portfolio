Repurposing an older Android device to act as a lightweight, low-power server.

The Setup Process

This involves setting up the right environment, managing battery usage, and configuring networking to access services remotely. Using an old phone rather than a Raspberry Pi saves e-waste and utilizes the built-in UPS (the battery).
