What I've Worked On

A deeper dive into some of the personal projects and experiments I've been working on recently.

Self Hosting on an old phone

Repurposing an older Android device to act as a lightweight, low-power server. This involves setting up the right environment, managing battery usage, and configuring networking to access services remotely.

Rooting and running alpine linux on a phone

Gaining root access to bypass manufacturer restrictions and deploying a complete Alpine Linux environment. This transforms the device into a highly capable and resource-efficient Linux machine.
